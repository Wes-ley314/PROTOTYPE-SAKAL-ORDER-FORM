Assistant settings
The assistant needs an Anthropic key to think with. Two ways to give it one.
1Open Connect sheet and make sure the script is in place.
2In Apps Script, go to Project Settings → Script Properties and add a property named ANTHROPIC_KEY with your key as the value.
3Redeploy as a new version. Every iPad and phone on the shop's sheet can then use the assistant, and the key never leaves Google.
